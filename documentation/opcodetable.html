CHIP-8 Instruction Logic & Macros Guide

Complete technical guide detailing precise pseudo-code implementation logic and macro definitions[cite: 1].

Disambiguated LD & Flow Macros

#define CLS                  0x00E0
#define RET                  0x00EE
#define JP(nnn)              (0x1000 | ((nnn) & 0x0FFF))
#define LD_IMM(x, nn)        (0x6000 | (((x)&0xF)<<8) | ((nn)&0xFF))
#define LD_REG(x, y)         (0x8000 | (((x)&0xF)<<8) | (((y)&0xF)<<4))
#define LD_INDEX(nnn)        (0xA000 | ((nnn) & 0x0FFF))

Timer & IO Macros

#define LD_VX_DT(x)          (0xF007 | (((x)&0xF)<<8))
#define LD_DT_VX(x)          (0xF015 | (((x)&0xF)<<8))
#define LD_ST_VX(x)          (0xF018 | (((x)&0xF)<<8))
#define DRW(x, y, n)         (0xD000 | (((x)&0xF)<<8) | (((y)&0xF)<<4) | ((n)&0xF))

8xy2AND_REG(x, y)Vx = Vx & Vy[cite: 1].
8xy3XOR_REG(x, y)Vx = Vx ^ Vy[cite: 1].

Opcode Table & Pseudo-Code Logic (Part 2)[cite: 1]

OpcodeMacro / MnemonicImplementation Logic (Pseudo-code)
8xy5SUB_REG(x, y)VF = (Vx >= Vy) ? 1 : 0; Vx = Vx - Vy[cite: 1].
8xy6SHR_REG(x, y)VF = Vx & 0x1; Vx = Vx >> 1[cite: 1].
8xy7SUBN_REG(x,y)VF = (Vy >= Vx) ? 1 : 0; Vx = Vy - Vx[cite: 1].
8xyESHL_REG(x, y)VF = (Vx >> 7) & 0x1; Vx = (Vx << 1) & 0xFF[cite: 1].

AnnnLD_INDEX(nnn)I = nnn[cite: 1].
BnnnJP_V0(nnn)PC = nnn + V0[cite: 1].
CxnnRND(x, nn)Vx = RandomByte() & nn[cite: 1].
DxynDRW(x, y, n)VF = 0; Loop rows n, fetch byte from memory[I+row], check pixel collision, XOR onto display[cite: 1].
Ex9ESKP(x)If KeyPressed(Vx), PC = PC + 2[cite: 1].
ExA1SKNP(x)If !KeyPressed(Vx), PC = PC + 2[cite: 1].

Fx33LD_BCD(x)mem[I]=Vx/100; mem[I+1]=(Vx/10)%10; mem[I+2]=Vx%10[cite: 1].
